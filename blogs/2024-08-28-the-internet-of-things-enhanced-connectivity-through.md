The Internet of Things (IoT) is transforming industries by enabling devices to communicate, collect, and exchange data seamlessly. This interconnected ecosystem relies on robust, high-speed communication technologies to function effectively. Photonics, which involves the use of light to transmit data, plays a critical role in enhancing the connectivity of IoT devices.
